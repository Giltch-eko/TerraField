# Recommendation methodology

Version 1.0 is a deterministic, illustrative heuristic over **21 crop profiles**, not ML, a probability, confidence estimate, yield forecast or a guarantee. The rule implementation and source links are in `node-server/engine.js`, `server/data/crops.json`, and [METHOD.md](METHOD.md).

For pH (weight 30), growing-season temperature (25) and annual rainfall (25), score 100 in the crop's optimal band, 0 outside the absolute range, and linearly interpolate between. Soil texture weighs 10 with simplified classes; humidity and season weigh 5 each using prototype bands/tags, not sourced local prescriptions. Unknown season/humidity and missing crop texture data are excluded and weights renormalized for each crop, so denominators differ. Score rounded to 0–100; if any of pH, temperature or rainfall falls outside an absolute band, score capped at 50. Sort by score, then crop name. Missing information and reasons appear in the result. A score may still look high with missing factors; it is not calibrated confidence.

Required user inputs are soil type, pH, annual rainfall and growing-season temperature; a season selection including `unknown` is required. N/P/K are recorded but not used in the score. Irrigation, water reliability, drainage, soil depth, pest risk, salinity, prices, local law, cultivar and climate trend are not scored. Current Open-Meteo weather does not provide annual rainfall or substitute for seasonal climate. SoilGrids is a modelled point, not a lab result. Input/source metadata is self-reported and may persist after individual values are manually edited; it is not a complete per-field provenance ledger. Examples are synthetic. Organic-first guidance is rule-based and conditional, prioritizes prevention and ecological practice, and must not be confused with certification, diagnosis, label approval or professional advice. Obtain local measurement and expert review before decisions. No farmer trial or independent predictive validation was performed.
