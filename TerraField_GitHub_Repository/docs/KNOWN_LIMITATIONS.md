# Known limitations

- **Offline global search is broken:** the exported source omitted GeoNames `place-meta.json.gz` and `world-places.tsv.gz`; one original test fails. Online search can fail/rate-limit; manual coordinates remain available.
- Map: EOX 2016/17 ~10 m historical mosaic, not live or survey-grade. No verified fallback base layer. OSM standard tile access was blocked in this environment; do not bypass or claim it works. Small fields, poles, antimeridian, holes and polygons >1° are not supported. Undo removes the last draft vertex or reverts a recent drag during edit, not arbitrary history across completed sessions. Dragging vertices needs a pointer; keyboard vertex entry/redraw is available.
- Plant catalogue: 2,302 distinct normalized historical names; no guarantee of 2,302 accepted species or locally cultivated varieties. Only 21 engine profiles and 21 matching catalogue rows. Zero records have both accepted name and family fields under the inspected criterion. No 20,000 sourced record delivery, expert reconciliation or training; importer does not by itself establish licences or cultivation.
- The model is unvalidated and deterministic, misses many agronomic factors, renormalizes missing data, and scores are not probabilities or yield guarantees. Annual rainfall and growing-season climate require actual user data. Provenance is self-reported, not per-field immutable lineage; editing one value may leave a conservative original source label. Soil model is not laboratory analysis. Weather and forecast services may fail; no historical climatology feed or future trend model is implemented.
- Organic advisor is general deterministic guidance, not locally validated instructions, dose, label, product registration, disease diagnosis, guaranteed safe input, crop-specific end-to-end plan or certification. No synthetic fertilizer or conventional synthetic pesticide recommendations are offered. Verify local restrictions and certification.
- SQLite and browser bearer secrets are not production multi-user authentication. Clearing browser storage, changing origin or losing the key loses access. Device/session storage and raw backups contain sensitive data. No cloud deployment, account security, real user trial, external penetration test, screen-reader audit, native Windows execution or Docker test completed.
- Print-to-PDF relies on the user's browser. Headless Chromium generated a PDF during testing; native dialogs and all printers are untested. CSV applies common spreadsheet formula guards but cannot promise every spreadsheet is safe.
- Original LICENSE, hero, Windows runtime and offline GeoNames data were missing from the export. Hero is generated and not the original. Confirm redistribution rights with the original owner. An older included presentation may not reflect these updates.
